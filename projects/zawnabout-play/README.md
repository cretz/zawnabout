# zawnabout-play

Gameplay, laid on top of the islands. It reads the world and one player's dragon and never changes either, so the islands are exactly the same without it. For now that is the hoard (finds to pick up by landing right beside one, sets of them that bring people, bunting or lanterns out when finished, and a lair the hoard heaps up in) and postcards: land at a beautiful spot and the view is painted onto a card (`paint.rs`), kept in the save as text. And people: named locals at their spots with lines and a favor each (give back a find, light a beacon, bring a postcard, fetch a lost thing, give a ride), paid in gold, gifts and town favor, the first time only. Townsfolk often ask something small (a ride, a dropped hat, a letter to deliver), just for fun: no gold, no favor, nothing to grind. And flying for fun (`flying.rs`): stunts named the first time they are done (gaps under bridges and arches, moves like long dives and glides, riding things that move), natural courses of gates flown in any order, and races against trains and boats with no clock. And stories (`story.rs`): campaigns that change the islands only while one runs, such as the lights going out island by island until their lamps are lit, set aside any time with progress kept in the save. Content comes from `world/first/play.kdl`, compiled at build time.

The game pulls it in behind its `play` cargo feature (on by default) and can switch it off while running (`play off`, native `--no-play`, web `?play=0`). Saves are plain text, a section a layer, keyed by the stable ids in `play.kdl`.

# Gameplay: Granny Pim climbs on for a ride up to the summit snowman, chats on the way,
# and hops off with thanks when the dragon lands there.
cards keep
goto-find pim 6
wait 0.3
expect can-talk
ui-key Enter
expect card
ui-key Enter
expect riding
shot aboard.png
goto -1600 600 120 0
wait-until say 20
wait 0.2
shot chat.png
ask-rider
expect card
shot ask.png
ui-key Escape
goto -1705 -415 0 0
wait-until card 5
expect not riding
expect helped pim
shot thanks.png

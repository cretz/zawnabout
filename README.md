# zawnabout

A cozy game about being a dragon. Fly over islands, swoop through caves, land wherever
you like, and see what you find.

**Play it in your browser at <https://zawnabout.quest>.**

## Take it easy

There is nothing to win and nothing to lose. No timers, no levels, no scores, no
grinding, and nobody to compete with. Fly wherever you like, for as long as you like.
If you want something to do, there is always something nearby.

## Things to do

- Fly anywhere: over towns and beaches, up mountains, through arches and waterfalls,
  and down into huge caves.
- Find treasures tucked away all over the islands, and pile them up in your hoard.
- Land somewhere beautiful to keep a postcard of it.
- Chat with the townsfolk, help them out, and give them rides on your back.
- Hop on a ferry, a train, a cable car or a roller coaster and enjoy the view.
- Meet the other dragons. One of them might trust you with an egg.

## Playing

It works with a keyboard, a gamepad, a mouse, or a touch screen. Open the journal for
the controls, and change any key you like.

Your progress is saved in your browser. There are no accounts, nothing to sign up for,
and nothing is ever sent anywhere.

The browser needs WebGPU: a recent Chrome or Edge, or Safari on an up to date iPad or
Mac.
